QBits: Quantum Harmonic Oscillator with Physics-Informed Neural Networks

A comprehensive framework for quantum simulation featuring approaches to quantum measurements through neural network-integrated Fourier transforms, learnable unitary transformations that build upon existing techniques, and the innovative Fourier Recurrent Unit (FRU). Demonstrates superior energy conservation with Lagrangian Neural Networks and includes advanced visualization tools for quantum dynamics.
